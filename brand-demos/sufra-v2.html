1

A sufra is the spread laid out for guests

We sell that feeling to restaurants: hospitality that starts at the table, in the guest's own language, and a calm system behind it for the people who run the floor.

Arabic is not a translation

Both scripts carry equal weight in the lockup and in every screen. Arabic copy is written for Gulf guests, not converted from English. Layouts are designed right to left, not mirrored at the end.

Quiet frame, real food

Interfaces stay neutral so the venue and its dishes lead. The colour lives in photography, the arch and one indigo action. Nothing glows, nothing floats for decoration.

Hosts, not robots

We speak like a good host: warm, brief, specific. We never say "AI", "seamless" or "successfully". We say what happened and what happens next.

The arch

An open majlis arch frames a cloche, both resting on the table line. The same arch frames every dish photo and marks every table on the floor plan. Those are its only three uses.

Construction

Grid
48 × 48 units. All strokes are 4 units.
Arch
Outer radius 16 units, centred on x 24, y 22. Legs drop straight to the table line at y 41.
Cloche
Half circle, radius 8, sitting on the table line, with a 3-unit handle.
Table line
From x 3 to x 45, so it overshoots the arch by 5 units each side. Round caps on this line only.
Wordmark
"Sufra" in Beiruti 700. "سفرة" in Beiruti 700, set to the same cap height as the Latin. Divider is a 1.5 px rule at 35% of the ink colour.
Status
Draft drawn in SVG. Before launch, a lettering pass to match the Arabic and Latin stroke weights by eye.

3

Indigo and pearl

Pearl paper, indigo ink, and a dried-lime ochre that appears in small doses: the current step, the "now" marker, a bill request, the top dish. Switch the theme in the contents rail to see the dark set. Values update from the tokens.

Pearl and surfaces 82%Ink 10%Indigo 6%Ochre 2%

Measured contrast

Ratios are calculated live from the current theme. Text needs 4.5:1, icons, focus rings and shapes need 3:1.

PairSampleRatioUse
  • Ochre is a shape colour on light. Any ochre words use --accent-text.
  • Status always pairs a colour with a word: New, Preparing, Ready, Late. Colour never carries meaning alone.
  • No hue gradients, glows or coloured side stripes on cards. Depth comes from hairlines, and in light mode one soft shadow on floating layers.

Two families, two scripts each

Beiruti carries names: the venue, dishes, table numbers. IBM Plex Sans and Plex Sans Arabic carry every interface word, price and figure. Both families were drawn for Arabic and Latin together, so mixed lines keep one rhythm.

Tabular figures: AED 1,284.50 · 12:45 · T12

IBM Plex Sans + Plex Sans Arabic 400, 500, 600 · interface · licence OFL

Scale

StepEnglishArabicSize / line height
  • Arabic never goes below 13 px. Latin 12 px is for chart axes only.
  • Arabic body line height is 1.75 against 1.55 for Latin, so diacritics never touch.
  • No letter-spacing on Arabic, ever. No tracking on Latin below 24 px.
  • Interface digits are Western (0–9) in both languages, with tabular figures for prices, times and counts.
  • Headings are always upright. Emphasis comes from weight and size, never italics.

The table card

The card is where the brand meets the guest first, so it carries the arch shape itself. Shown here at real size on most screens.

Format
Folded tent, 100 × 150 mm per face, arch top with 50 mm radius. Same face front and back.
Bleed and safe area
3 mm bleed on all edges. Keep text and QR 6 mm inside the trim. The hairline frame sits 3 mm in.
QR
32 mm printed, never under 25 mm, with a quiet zone of 4 modules. Indigo on pearl only. Each table gets its own random link, never the table number.
Venue
The venue name in Beiruti, English and Arabic, sits low on the card. Sufra appears once, small, at the foot.
Table number
Beiruti 700 at 22 mm, with "طاولة 7" in Beiruti 600 underneath.
Call to scan
English and Arabic, both visible, same size. Plex Sans 3.4 mm, Plex Sans Arabic 3.6 mm.
Table code
Only when the venue switches on "Require table code" in Settings.
Stock
350 gsm uncoated or soft-touch matte. No gloss: it reflects table lamps into the camera.
Files
Exported per table from Tables & QR in the owner app, three per A4 sheet.

The three apps

These frames are the live prototype, not pictures. Everything runs on the same tokens. Open the full prototype to order as a guest and watch the ticket arrive on the staff board.

Guest · phone

  • The venue leads. Its name, its photography, its table number. Sufra appears once, small, on the welcome screen.
  • Dish photos sit in the arch. Dishes without a photo get no placeholder; the row closes up.
  • Thumb-zone actions: the order bar and primary buttons live at the bottom, 48 px tall.
  • After sending, the guest follows the order step by step and can call a waiter or ask for the bill without waving.
Open the prototype

Staff · the pass

  • Table number first and largest, quantities before dish names, one action per ticket.
  • Late tickets say "late" in words; nothing flashes. Calls and bill requests sit above the rail.
  • Light and dark from the same tokens. Dark is the default on a kitchen tablet.

Owner · home

  • A left menu like any serious admin: Home, Live board, Orders, then Menu, Tables & QR, Team, Settings.
  • Figures are separated by hairlines, not boxes. One chart, one floor plan, the latest orders and the top dishes.
  • Every figure on this page is computed from the prototype's sample orders and is labelled sample data.

Speak like a host

Warm, brief and specific, in both languages. Say what happened and what happens next. Arabic is written for Gulf guests in clear Modern Standard Arabic with a Gulf ear, never word-for-word from English.

We say

  • Your order is with the kitchen.طلبك عند المطبخ الآن.
  • Take your time. We will tell you when it is ready.خذ راحتك. سنخبرك حين يجهز.
  • Someone is on their way.أحد الطاقم في طريقه إليك.
  • Sold out todayنفد اليوم

We never say

  • Order placed successfully!تم إرسال الطلب بنجاح!

  • Seamless next-gen dining experience

8

Motion and states

One easing curve, three durations. Motion confirms an action or shows where something came from; it never decorates. With reduced motion on, everything becomes a short fade.

120 ms

Press, hover and toggle feedback. Buttons press to 98%. Colours shift; nothing jumps.

200 ms

State changes: a ticket arrives with a fade and a soft highlight that clears after 4 seconds.

320 ms

Sheets and drawers: the guest item sheet rises from the bottom, the owner editor slides in from the side.

  • Easing: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) everywhere. No bounce, no spring.
  • Every control ships default, hover, focus, pressed and disabled states. Forms add error text under the field and keep the space for it, so nothing jumps.
  • Focus rings are 2 px, indigo on light and ochre on dark, and appear instantly.
  • Success is silent when the result is visible: the ticket moves, the switch flips. Messages are kept for failures and for things the user cannot see.
  • Sound is opt-in: the staff board chimes only after "Shift on", which also keeps the tablet awake.

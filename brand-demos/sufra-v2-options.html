Palettes

Each has a light and a dark ramp. Surfaces stay quiet; the brand colour carries the guest welcome and headers, the ink colour carries actions. Ratios are measured live from the tokens.

1 · Majlis dusk

v1 refined. Stone paper, roasted-date ink, night indigo, one saffron spark. The most Emirati.

2 · Qahwa and cardamom

Pearl paper, coffee ink, deep cardamom green, brass. Nothing a delivery app would wear.

3 · Indigo and pearl

Indigo leads as brand and action, dried-lime ochre as the spark. Coolest and most hotel-grade.

The mark

An open majlis arch frames a cloche, both resting on the table line. The same arch frames every dish photo and marks every table on the floor plan.

  • Arch radius 16 units, legs land on the table line. Cloche radius 8, centred.
  • The table line overshoots the arch by 5 units each side, with round caps only there.
  • One colour at a time. Never a gradient, outline or shadow.

Brand guidelines · Direction C
The idea

The best rooms feel attended, never served. We put the maître d's attention in the guest's hand, and keep the room quiet.

  1. Discretion

    No notification sounds for guests. No badges. The app whispers.

  2. Ceremony

    Menus read like a printed carte: courses, not categories.

  3. The venue leads

    Our ring sits small at the foot. The restaurant's name is set large.

01 · The idea
A sufra is the spread laid out for guests. We sell that feeling to restaurants: hospitality that starts at the table, in the guest's own language.
Arabic is not a translation

Both scripts carry equal weight in every lockup. RTL is designed, not mirrored.

Warmth over speed

We never say "fast" first. We say welcomed, looked after, remembered.

Craft you can feel

Arch geometry, paper texture, considered spacing. Nothing glossy.

02 · Mark & lockup

The arch

A majlis doorway and a serving cloche in one shape, resting on the table line. It frames the QR on every table, so the mark and the product are the same object.

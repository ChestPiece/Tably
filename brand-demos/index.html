Three ways to sell a feeling, not a food app

Each direction is a full brand system for the ordering platform: name, mark, colour, type, the table card, the guest screen and the voice. Names are working proposals. QR codes are visual placeholders and do not scan. Dishes and venues are samples.

My pick

Sufra as the brand, with Covers' discipline inside the staff tools. Sufra is the only one a competitor from London or San Francisco cannot copy: a bilingual lockup, an arch that is both the logo and the QR frame, and a voice built on hospitality, which is what owners in this market actually sell.

Maître looks the most expensive, but it narrows you to fine dining. Covers is the most flexible as a platform, but it sells efficiency, which is the feeling you said you do not want to lead with.

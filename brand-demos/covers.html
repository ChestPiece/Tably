01Idea

Premium is precision. A great restaurant never loses a ticket, never guesses a table, never makes a guest wave twice. We are the pass, made visible.

Exact

Real times, real counts. Minutes in mono. No vague "soon".

Calm under load

Saturday at 9pm looks as ordered as Tuesday at 6.

Out of the way

The venue's food is the hero. We are the grid it sits on.

02Wordmark

A word and a light

Condensed Archivo Black at its narrowest. The red dot is the pass light: an order is ready. It is the only red in the system and it always means "now".

PrimaryCovers
ReversedCovers
App markC

Dot diameter = stem width. Sits on the cap line, one stem away from the s.

Minimum: 64px wide on screen, 18mm in print.

Never widen the font, never colour the letters, never use the dot as decoration.
